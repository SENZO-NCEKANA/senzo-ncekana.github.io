Why PharmaGuard exists

hydralazine — lowers blood pressure hydroxyzine — an antihistamine

Eleven characters each. They differ in three. In a dispensary at speed, on a handwritten script, that is the whole margin — which is why the industry answer is Tall Man lettering: capitalise the characters that differ, so the eye is forced to see them. Five years of catching that by hand is why I built a system that catches it instead.
