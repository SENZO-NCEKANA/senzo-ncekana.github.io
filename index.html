About

From the dispensary to AI & data

I’m an AI/ML engineer and data analyst with a clinical foundation — a certified Pharmacy Technician with 3+ years in regulated healthcare. That background shapes how I work: a focus on data integrity, control, and systems people can actually trust — the same discipline that risk, audit, and compliance work demands.

On the engineering side, I built PharmaGuard — a production-ready, multi-agent platform for pharmaceutical similarity detection using Google Gemini, LangChain, FAISS, and FastAPI. On the analytics side, I build reusable ETL and machine-learning workflows that turn raw, multi-source data into anomaly detection, risk scoring, and decision-ready dashboards. I work across Python, SQL, R, and the modern AI stack, and I’m now focused on bringing that toolkit into banking and financial services.
